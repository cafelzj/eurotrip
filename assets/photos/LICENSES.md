# Local photographs and attribution

Photographs are served as local JPEG/PNG files. Food and product photographs in the current catalog have individual visual-review records; source and license verification alone does not establish visual approval. No food images were generated.

Each image retains its original author and license. Wikimedia Commons and Open Food Facts are separate providers. Open Food Facts product photographs are licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://world.openfoodfacts.org/legal); the database licenses are not used as photograph licenses. Resizing, orientation/profile normalization and format encoding are the only local changes. Existing full license text copies remain alongside their corresponding media.
